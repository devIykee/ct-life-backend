# Hackathon Evaluation: Base44 × Nitro/Overwolf ($65k)

**Status:** INELIGIBLE — Formally Excluded from Submission  
**Audit Date:** November 2026  

---

## 1. Summary of Rules Review

The Base44 × Nitro/Overwolf $65,000 contest was evaluated against the official competition terms and conditions (specifically Sections 6.1 and 7.2):

1. **Development Timeline:** The competition rules mandate that all entries must be built entirely anew using the Base44 development platform between November 5 and December 3, 2026. Pre-existing games, external engines, reskins, or wrappers are explicitly disqualified.
2. **Monetization & Wagering Prohibitions:** The contest terms strictly prohibit games that require payment, in-app purchases, or login to play, and specifically ban real-money wagering or gambling-related mechanics.
3. **Engine Requirements:** Submissions must run natively on Base44 web hosting rather than standalone Unity WebGL export builds.

---

## 2. Determination

CT Life is a pre-existing Unity/WebGL multiplayer game featuring an integrated multi-chain economy and the Rug Room casino. Attempting to package CT Life into a Base44 wrapper would directly violate contest eligibility terms and risk disqualification.

**Action:** CT Life will **not** be submitted to the Base44 contest. Development and launch focus remain dedicated to the live game launch, the WIF Hackathon on Robinhood Chain, and the NEAR Hackathon.
