# Map Travel (phase 1)

Capability **`travel`**, off by default. Until an operator adds it to `CAPABILITIES_ON`, every
route below answers `503 CAPABILITY_OFF {capability: travel}`, `GET /config` sends `travel: null`,
street rooms ignore `tripId`, and nothing in production changes.

A player on CT Island picks a venue on the map and gets three ways there: **walk** (free; the
client auto-paths at sprint speed, which stays inside the street room's 6.5 m/s rule, so nothing is
booked), **bus** and **taxi** (paid rides, priced and timed by the server). A ride ends on the
**street outside the destination door**, never inside a venue: walking in is the normal handoff,
and the venue's own join gate (PR #80) still decides entry.

## Defaults (`contract/config/travel.json`, all tunable without a client release)

These are pending product decisions; change the file, not code.

| Setting                             | Default                                                   | Meaning                                                                                                                                                |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| bus                                 | $2 flat, `12 s + d / 45 m/s`                              | picks up only at a bus stop (within `busStopRadiusM` 25 m)                                                                                             |
| taxi                                | $4 + $0.80 per 100 m, min $5, cap $15, `8 s + d / 65 m/s` | picks up anywhere                                                                                                                                      |
| `unlockNetWorthCents`               | 0 for both                                                | "priced by wealth" = richer players unlock better modes; prices stay flat                                                                              |
| `roadFactor`                        | 1.3                                                       | distance = straight line from your server position to the door x 1.3                                                                                   |
| `minDistanceM`                      | 60                                                        | closer: rides are not offered (`TOO_CLOSE`), walk                                                                                                      |
| `gatedBooking`                      | `warn`                                                    | a gated destination is ridden to the street outside the rope; `refuse` = 403 `VENUE_GATED` before any charge                                           |
| `arrivalOffsetM`                    | 4.6                                                       | arrival = door + 4.6 m outward (rope margin 0.6 m + 4 m), facing the door                                                                              |
| `cooldownSec` / `tripsPerHour`      | 10 / 30                                                   | `429 TRAVEL_COOLDOWN` with `Retry-After`                                                                                                               |
| `quoteTtlSec` / `bookDriftM`        | 60 / 20                                                   | a quote lives 60 s; booking needs you within 20 m of where you were quoted                                                                             |
| `ticketGraceSec` / `arriveEarlySec` | 120 / 1                                                   | the arrival ticket opens 1 s before `arriveAt` and closes 120 s after it                                                                               |
| `busStops`                          | 11                                                        | the 2 shelters islandgen builds downtown plus one kerb-side stop per district, generated by the frontend's `tools/world/islandgen/export_bus_stops.py` |

Rides are private: other players see you leave your street room and appear at the destination,
not the ride. The classic free instant "Go" teleport is untouched; capability
**`travelInstantGoOff`** only tells the client to stop offering it while `travel` is on (off by
default, so instant Go stays). Closing the teleport on the server is phase 2.

## Flow

1. `GET /v1/travel/quote?to=<venueId>`: the server reads the caller's last accepted island position
   (`ctl:pos`, at most 30 s old, else `409 TRAVEL_NOT_IN_WORLD`), measures to the venue door in
   `contract/ids/districts.json`, and returns every mode with fare, duration and why it is not
   available, plus the door's `gate`. The quote is stored in Redis (`ctl:travel:quote:<id>`).
2. `POST /v1/travel/trips {quoteId, mode}` (Idempotency-Key): one transaction like the Duo Shop
   order. Lock the balance row (`FOR UPDATE`, serialising the player's bookings), return the
   existing trip for a replayed `travel-trip:<user>:<key>` ledger ref, check the open trip (a
   partial unique index allows one `booked` trip per player), cooldown, hourly limit, unlock and
   cash, insert `travel_trips`, debit, write the ledger row (`travel_fare`). After the commit the
   single-use ticket `ctl:travel:<userId>` `{tripId, district, x, z, yaw, arriveAt, expiresAt}` is
   written. CT Jail (`requireNotJailed`) and the Barn (`SYBIL_JAILED`) refuse.
3. The client leaves its street (and venue) room and plays the ride. While the ticket is open, a
   street join without it is refused (`4409 TRAVEL_IN_TRANSIT`).
4. At `arriveAt` the client joins the street room of `arrival.district` with `{tripId}`. Early is
   `4425 TRAVEL_NOT_ARRIVED` (ticket kept); used, expired, foreign or wrong district is
   `4410 TRAVEL_TICKET_INVALID`. On success the ticket is deleted atomically, the player is placed
   and shown at the arrival point, `ctl:pos` is written there and the trip becomes `arrived`
   (docs/REALTIME.md "Map Travel arrival").
5. Cancel before `arriveAt`: `POST /v1/travel/trips/{id}/cancel`, full refund (`travel_refund`),
   ticket deleted. A ticket never used is refunded by the **travel-sweep** worker every 30 s
   (`expired_refunded`), exactly once (`travel-refund:<tripId>` is unique in the ledger). An
   arrived trip is never refunded.
6. App killed mid-ride: `GET /v1/travel/trips/current` returns the open trip to finish or cancel.

## Known gaps

- `ore_refinery`'s door is about 70 m outside every district polygon in `districts.json`, so no
  street room can hold a player there: rides to it are quoted `NOT_SERVED` (walk only) until the
  frontend's district export covers it.
- No analytics event yet (the `analytics_events` CHECK would need a migration; travel analytics is
  phase 5). Homes are not destinations yet (their doors are not in the contract).
- Phase 2 (capability `travelAuthority`): venue rooms refuse a join whose entrance is beyond reach
  of a fresh position, the lobby/app-start entry rule, comp tickets for game-sponsored trips.
